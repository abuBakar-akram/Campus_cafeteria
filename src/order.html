Campus Cafeteria Ordering Guide

Learn how to select items, complete payments, and collect your food smoothly.

1. How to Choose Food Items

Selecting your meal at the campus cafeteria is quick and easy:

  • Browse the daily updated menu displayed on digital screens or physical cards at the entrance.
  • Choose from various categories including fast food, traditional meals, snacks, and beverages.
  • Note down your chosen item codes or inform the counter staff directly.

2. Counter Payment & Order-Token Collection

Ensure a smooth checkout process by following these payment steps:

  • Proceed to the billing counter once you have finalized your order.
  • Make your payment using cash or accepted digital mobile banking payment apps.
  • Collect your printed or digital Order Token. Keep this token safe as it is mandatory for food pickup.

3. Peak Hours & Food Pickup Rules

Guidelines to manage rush times efficiently:

  • Peak Hours: 12:30 PM – 2:30 PM (Expect higher waiting times due to student rush after classes).
  • Keep an eye on the digital token display screen installed near the pickup counter.
  • Present your token at the pickup window only when your unique token number flashes on the screen to collect your order.
